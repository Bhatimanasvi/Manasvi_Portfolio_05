Divjot Bag Brand Case Studies: The marketing challenges, creative strategy executed, and bottom-line impact generated.

Playful Reel Target: Parents & Kids

Jerry Found His Favourite Bag

⚠️ The Problem:

Low organic engagement on standard bag posts; parents were skipping generic product ads.

💡 My Strategy:

Integrated nostalgia & popular pop-culture character ("Jerry") into short storytelling format to capture immediate attention.

🚀 The Result:

Higher retention & +40% increase in DMs asking for kids' collection link.

Product Showcase Target: Daily Commuters

Premium Quality Backpack

⚠️ The Problem:

Customers were skeptical about product durability and compartment space from static photos.

💡 My Strategy:

Shot close-up feature callouts (zipper action, padding, water resistance) with fast-paced video edits.

🚀 The Result:

Cleared product feature doubts, resulting in 2.5x higher landing page click-throughs.

Lifestyle Reel Target: Gen-Z Students

Campus & Daily Commute

⚠️ The Problem:

Brand lacked a distinct aesthetic connect with Gen-Z college students looking for trendy bags.

💡 My Strategy:

Produced a high-vibe campus aesthetic lifestyle shoot using trending audio and relatable college outfit pairings.

🚀 The Result:

Organic shares boosted reel reach by 300% without paid ad spend.

New Arrival Drop Target: Back to School

Dino & Space Collection

⚠️ The Problem:

New inventory drop needed fast sales momentum before the school season peak ended.

💡 My Strategy:

Designed an urgent "Limited Stock / Back-to-School" teaser video with vibrant color highlights and strong CTA overlay.

🚀 The Result:

Driven high initial launch excitement, resulting in stock clearance in under 2 weeks.
