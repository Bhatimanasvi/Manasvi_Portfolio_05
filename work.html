Reel Format 01 Kids & Parent Audience

Pop-Culture Character "Jerry" Reel

⚠️ The Problem:

Low organic engagement on standard bag posts; parents were skipping generic product ads.

💡 My Strategy:

Integrated nostalgia & popular pop-culture character ("Jerry") into short storytelling format to capture immediate attention.

🚀 The Verified Result:

Turned low initial engagement into active viewer interest with higher watch times and steady DM inquiries.

📹 Visual Reel Hook: "Nostalgic Jerry Character Cut" Watch Time ↑
Reel Format 02 Product Feature Focus

Backpack Durability & Feature Close-ups

⚠️ The Problem:

Customers were skeptical about product durability and compartment space from static photos alone.

💡 My Strategy:

Shot close-up feature callouts (zipper action, padding, water resistance) with fast-paced video edits.

🚀 The Verified Result:

Answered key customer questions directly through visuals, guiding viewers smoothly from reel to website.

📹 Visual Reel Hook: "Zipper & Water Resistance Test" Confidence ↑
Reel Format 03 Gen-Z College Audience

Commute & Campus Outfit Pairings

⚠️ The Problem:

Brand lacked a distinct aesthetic connect with Gen-Z college students looking for trendy bags.

💡 My Strategy:

Produced a high-vibe campus aesthetic lifestyle shoot using trending audio and relatable college outfit pairings.

🚀 The Verified Result:

Organic shares extended reel reach effectively without relying on paid ad spend.

📹 Visual Reel Hook: "College GRWM & Bag Styling" Organic Shares ↑
Reel Format 04 Limited Drop Launch

Back-To-School Collection Teaser

⚠️ The Problem:

New inventory drop needed fast sales momentum before the school season peak ended.

💡 My Strategy:

Designed an urgent "Limited Stock / Back-to-School" teaser video with vibrant color highlights and strong CTA overlay.

🚀 The Verified Result:

Generated strong audience buzz early in the launch window to accelerate inventory movement.

📹 Visual Reel Hook: "Fast-Paced Multi-Bag Teaser" Launch Buzz ↑
